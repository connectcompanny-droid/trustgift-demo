Privacy Policy

Demo privacy notice · version 2026-11-v1

Information used

When you register, the service processes your e-mail address, first and last name, username, country, referral code, and the date and versions of your legal acknowledgements. Phone and WhatsApp numbers are optional. If you use the service, it also processes referral status, support messages and attachments, selected payment-method preferences, and your leaderboard opt-in choice.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, operate profiles and referrals, show the optional leaderboard, and provide support. Payment preferences are labels only: this demo does not transmit or persist wallet addresses, Pix keys, bank details, or payment credentials.

Visibility and providers

Your private profile and contacts are limited to you and authorized support agents. The public leaderboard shows only the username, country, and confirmed referral count of members who opt in. Supabase provides authentication, database, and private file storage for this demo.

Browser storage and retention

Your language choice is saved in this browser. Authentication uses Supabase session storage. Account data and support content remain in the configured Supabase project until deleted under its retention and backup settings; the operator should define and publish a deletion and retention process before launch.

Your choices and contact

You can opt out of the leaderboard in your profile and can contact Support through the app. A production launch must add the operator's contact details and explain how to exercise applicable privacy rights.

This notice is a demo summary, not legal advice. It must be reviewed and completed for the operator's actual practices and applicable laws before public launch.
